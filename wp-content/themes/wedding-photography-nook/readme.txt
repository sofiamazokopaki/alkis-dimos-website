== Description ==

Wedding Photography Nook is a full site editing (FSE) block theme built for wedding, engagement, and elopement photographers who want a fast, professional website without a hassle of a separate page builder. It works just as well for portrait, family, and lifestyle photography studios looking for the same clean, image-first design. Soft color palettes and elegant typography keep the focus on your photography rather than the interface, and because everything runs on native WordPress blocks, editing your homepage, about page, or services page takes minutes in the Site Editor — no shortcodes, no bloated plugin stack, no separate builder to learn. It comes with dedicated About, Services, Testimonials, and Gallery sections ready to fill in, plus a clean blog layout for behind-the-scenes posts or client stories. The theme is mobile responsive, translation-ready, and supports RTL languages, and being block-native means it plays nicely with the plugins you already use, without needing a separate builder. Whether you're building out a wedding photography brand or a broader portrait and family photography site, Wedding Photography Nook gives you a polished starting point without the technical overhead.
